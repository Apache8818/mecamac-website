Privacy notice

What this website does — and does not do — with your information. Last updated September 2026.

Who we are

Mecamac is an electromechanical contractor headquartered at Block 293, Way 9307, Al Misfah Industrial Area, Muscat, Sultanate of Oman. Questions about this notice can be sent to info@mecamac.com.

What this website collects

Nothing is stored on our servers when you browse. The site has no user accounts, no comment system and no advertising. The consultation form on the Contact page does not submit anything to us: it composes a message from what you type and opens it in your own WhatsApp, where you decide whether to send it. Calling, emailing or messaging us then happens through those services, under their own terms.

Preferences saved in your browser

Your choice of language (English or Arabic) and display mode (light or dark) is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it next time. This stays on your device, is never sent to us, and is cleared when you clear your browser data.

Third-party services on the site

Some elements are delivered by third parties, which may receive your IP address and standard browser information when they load: Google Fonts (typefaces), Google Maps (the embedded map on the Contact page) and WhatsApp (when you choose to message us). We do not currently use analytics or tracking cookies; if that changes, this notice will say so before it takes effect.

What we do with an enquiry

When you contact us, we use the details you provide to answer you and, where relevant, to prepare a technical response or proposal. Enquiry records are kept for as long as needed to serve the project and meet our legal and contractual obligations in the Sultanate of Oman, and are not sold or shared for marketing.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about your enquiry, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or ask us to stop contacting you. Write to info@mecamac.com and we will respond.
