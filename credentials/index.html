ISO9001:2015

Quality management — ISO 9001:2015

Our quality management system is certified to ISO 9001:2015 and covers design coordination, procurement, installation, testing and commissioning and after-sales support across all four disciplines. The certificate and its current scope are issued with every tender submission.
