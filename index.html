About Mecamac

What we are building towards, and how we work

Founded in Muscat in 2010 as a specialist electromechanical contractor, Mecamac has grown into four disciplines under one roof — smart buildings, HVAC, MEP contracting and mission-critical data centres — with its own factory, warehousing and engineering team in Al Misfah.

01

Vision

To be the GCC's reference contractor for buildings that cannot fail — where power, cooling and control are engineered as one system and proven before handover.

02

Mission

We design, build, commission and support electromechanical and building-automation systems for owners, consultants and main contractors in Oman and the GCC — holding the principal brands directly, fabricating in-country, and standing behind every installation through its working life.

03

How we work

  • Engineering first — decisions are made on calculation and test results, not on habit.
  • One accountable contractor — the same team designs, installs, commissions and supports.
  • In-country capability — stock, fabrication and engineers in Muscat, not a call centre abroad.
  • Proven, not promised — witnessed testing and documented handover on every package.

Working with Mecamac

What tender committees ask us

What is your prequalification standing and what do you submit at tender?

We maintain ISO 9001:2015 certification with current commercial registration, classification and insurance cover. Submissions include company and financial profile, project references with client contacts, key personnel CVs, HSE record, method statements and manufacturer authorisation letters for specified equipment.

See our credentials and what the pack contains ↗

Who do you work with, and in what role?

Consultants, main contractors, EPC firms, developers, owners and facility operators — across the full chain. Most often we hold the MEP and specialist systems package under a main contractor; we also contract directly with owners and developers, and take main contracts where the scope suits it. The role and interface boundaries are stated at tender.

Why does holding four disciplines in one company matter commercially?

Because most cost and programme loss on complex projects occurs at the interfaces between packages. When automation, HVAC, electrical and construction sit with one contractor, those interfaces are internal — coordinated in design rather than negotiated as variations during installation.

Can you deliver inside live, operational facilities?

Yes. We have executed MEP works inside operating colocation data centre halls without interrupting tenant service, governed by phasing plans, isolation permits, agreed windows and the operator's change-control regime. The same discipline applies to occupied hotels, offices and institutional buildings.

How do principal partnerships benefit the client rather than just the contractor?

Direct distribution agreements give factory warranty, technical backing from the manufacturer, in-country stock that protects the programme, and authorisation letters that satisfy tender requirements. It also means the party specifying the equipment is the party accountable for its performance.
